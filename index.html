About this comparison

This illustrative model compares local ground elevation with a selected sea-level rise. It does not determine whether ocean water could physically reach the location.

The +70 m setting is the app’s all-land-ice-melt scenario, not a date-based forecast. The 2 m figure is a scale reference, not an average adult height.

The water ripples around a fixed measurement line. The ruler, person and ground all share the same scale. The cliff is a schematic cross-section, not a reconstruction of local terrain.

Address searches are sent to Cesium ion and OpenStreetMap’s Nominatim service. Device location is used only when you choose “Use my location”. Location coordinates are included in the page URL to let you share a result.
