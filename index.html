About this map

This is a “bathtub” model: it fills every point of land that sits below the line you choose, whether or not the ocean could physically reach it. It is an approximation for illustration, not a survey or a flood-risk assessment.

+70 m is the sea level if all land ice on Earth melted. It says nothing about when — realistic rise this century is under about a metre.
